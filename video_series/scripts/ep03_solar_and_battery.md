# Episode 03 - Solar and the real battery (~60 s)
Screen: asset cards "PV" and "BESS", then "PV routing and curtailment" tab.

The plant also has five megawatts of floating solar, on the reservoir.
Solar is free energy, but you cannot control when it comes.
So the sun can go to three places: sold to the market, stored in the battery, or used to pump water.
If none of these is good, the plant must curtail, which means waste it.
The battery is small: one megawatt, two megawatt-hours. It is fast, but it fills quickly.
Its job is to move a few hours of energy to a better price, and to react fast.
