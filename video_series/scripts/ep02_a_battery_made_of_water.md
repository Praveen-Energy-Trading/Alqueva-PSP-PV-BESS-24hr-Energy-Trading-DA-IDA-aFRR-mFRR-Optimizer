# Episode 02 - A battery made of water (~70 s)
Screen: asset cards "Turbines" and "Pumps", then Reservoir trajectory tab.

How do you store electricity at the size of a power plant? With water.
When power is cheap, the pumps lift water from the lower lake to the upper lake.
When power is expensive, the water falls through the turbines and makes electricity.
Here, the turbines give five hundred and eighteen megawatts. The pumps use four hundred and forty-six.
The upper reservoir is Alqueva. The lower one is Pedrogao, and it is small.
That small lake is the catch: pump for too long and it runs out of room.
So the system must plan the water, hour by hour, not only the price.
