# Episode 01 - The plant in one minute (~65 s)
Screen: Overview page, the five asset cards at the top.

Every day, a power plant in Portugal must decide what to sell, before it knows tomorrow's prices.
I built the system that decides. Let me show you, one minute at a time.
This is the Alqueva plant. Five cards.
Turbines: four, of one hundred and twenty-nine megawatts each.
Pumps: four, that move water back uphill.
Floating solar: five megawatts peak.
A battery: one megawatt, two megawatt-hours.
And a safety duty called FCR: five megawatts always kept free, to help the grid frequency.
Together, five hundred and twenty-four megawatts.
Next episode: why a pump and a turbine make a battery made of water.
I am moving into electricity markets, and I would love to hear from you.
