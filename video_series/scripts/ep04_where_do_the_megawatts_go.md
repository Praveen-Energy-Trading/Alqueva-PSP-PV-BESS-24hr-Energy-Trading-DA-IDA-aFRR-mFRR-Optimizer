# Episode 04 - Where do the megawatts go? (~70 s)
Screen: "Allocation over the markets" bar.

One plant, many places to sell.
Most of it, about three hundred and forty megawatts, goes to the day-ahead market. That is the main auction.
Up to one hundred and five megawatts can be offered to aFRR, a fast reserve.
Up to seventy-four to mFRR, a slower reserve.
And five megawatts stay free for FCR.
In reserve markets you are paid for being ready, and paid again if the grid calls you.
But the same water and the same battery cannot be sold twice.
That is why this is an optimization problem, not a simple rule.
