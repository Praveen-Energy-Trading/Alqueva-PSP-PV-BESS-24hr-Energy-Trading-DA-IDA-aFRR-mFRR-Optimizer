# Video series plan: short daily episodes

Platform: LinkedIn only. Square video 1080x1080, 45-90 s (150-220 words), large burned-in subtitles (most people watch muted).
One idea per episode, hook in the first 3 seconds, a one-line "next episode" at the end. Post Mon-Fri mornings, one episode a day.
Post text: 2-line hook, 3 short bullets, one question, 3-5 hashtags, "Voice generated with AI". GitHub link in the FIRST COMMENT.
Voice: AI-generated (edge-tts). Call to action: one short sentence in every episode; a fuller ask in the last episode of each block.
